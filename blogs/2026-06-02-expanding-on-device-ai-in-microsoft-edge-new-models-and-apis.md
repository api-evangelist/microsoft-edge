Microsoft announced three updates to its on-device AI capabilities in Edge, including a developer preview of the smaller Aion-1.0-Instruct language model, new Language Detector and Translator APIs that support 145+ languages, and experimental on-device speech recognition. These features enable developers to build AI-powered web experiences using browser-built models without relying on cloud services or specialized hardware.
