Do you use a keyboard to navigate websites? If so, you've probably run into countless accessibility issues where groups of inputs or widgets are not easy, or even possible to get to with just the keyboard. A keyboard-accessible website lets you easily reach its various sections and then navigate within them in an intuitive way. Unfortunately, inaccessible websites are common on the internet. The Web Almanac reports that about 50% of websites don't use tabindex, a common technique for making keyboard navigation possible on the web. The issue is complex, though.
