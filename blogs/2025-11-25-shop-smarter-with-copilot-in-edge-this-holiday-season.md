The holiday season is upon us and with that comes a lot of excitement, planning, and of course, shopping. Between planning parties and putting up decorations, where is the time to go down your shopping list? Microsoft Edge is here to help with a new streamlined shopping experience to add cheer to your holidays. Your favorite shopping features – Cashback, Price comparison, Price history, Product insights, and Price tracking – are now coming to Copilot in Edge to make shopping even smarter.
